musixverse

the ambitious first venture

project scope: highly ambitious web3 platform

team size: led a team of 15 people

goal: disrupt the music industry

why musixverse failed

01
wrong business model for the market

it needed funding, network, or deep experience. we had none of the three.

02
zero external funding to sustain a large team

most of the team couldn't go all in around day jobs and college.

03
high web3 friction for everyday users

users had to learn too much before they could even use the product.

04
took too long to ship and do market validation

early traction never picked up.

choosing your path

the business model you pick is a life you pick.

the vc-backed path
primary goal

hypergrowth, market dominance, big exit

trade-off

dilution, loss of control, board oversight

best for

capital-intensive, winner-take-all markets

the bootstrapped path
primary goal

profitability, ownership, freedom

trade-off

slower growth, personal financial risk

best for

self-serve products, long-term control

ask yourself

do you value scale or control?

building a product to sell, or a business to own?

what is success: money or freedom?

don't do it.

it's hard. going the vc way is easier.
