self-reflection

  • primary goal: hypergrowth, market dominance, and 'big exit' (ipo/acquisition)
  • trade-off: equity dilution, loss of control, board oversight, high-pressure scaling
  • best for: capital-intensive ideas, winner-take-all markets, desire for venture-scale impact
  • primary goal: profitability, founder ownership, freedom, and sustainable lifestyle
  • trade-off: slower initial growth, personal financial risk, limited 'war chest'
  • best for: self-serve digital products, autonomy, desire for long-term control
  • do you value scale or control more?
  • are you building a product to sell or a business to own?
  • what is your definition of success? (money vs. freedom)
